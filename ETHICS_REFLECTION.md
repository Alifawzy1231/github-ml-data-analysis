Question 1: Why is it important to verify data collected from public APIs?

It is important to verify data collected from public APIs because the data may contain errors, missing values, duplicates, or outdated information.



Question 2: Why should data analysts document the source of their data?

Data analysts should document the source of their data so that others can understand where the data came from and verify its reliability.



Question 3: How can missing or inaccurate data affect data analysis and decision-making?

Missing or inaccurate data can produce misleading results and incorrect conclusions. This can negatively affect decision-making.
